What it is

A bunch of Godalming people getting stuck into projects that make our town more creative, more resilient and more ours.

Empty shop windows. Fields eyed up for houses. Bills that only go one way. Rivers full of cr@p.

We keep being told it's an emergency, and most days it doesn't feel like one.

So this is a quieter kind of change. We could moan about it, or we could get going. Doing it now costs less than doing it later.

And doing it together is a lot more fun.

Highs, lows and the bit in between+

We play with the highs. We sit with the lows.

This isn't about doing more and more. It's about knowing when to stop, breathe, regroup and come back down to earth.

We can only build far, and build well, from a good place. So we follow the seasons, the moon and our own energy. Some weeks we make things happen. Some weeks we rest and let things settle.

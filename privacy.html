Your data, in plain words

Va Va Voom is a small local experiment, started by Kev, Tim and Carl of the Better Men group in Godalming. Here's exactly what we keep and why.

What we collect, and when

  • Where's home: the town or village you type, so we can show what's near you.
  • What pulls you: the themes you tap and the things you'd come to, so we can plan what people round here want.
  • When you say "I'm in": we count it. If you choose to leave your first name and a mobile or email, the host can let you know about that event. That part is optional.
  • If you share something or offer to help: what you tell us, your first name and how to reach you.
  • Feedback: whatever you choose to tell us.
  • How the site's used: only if you say yes. We use PostHog to see which bits work. Anything you type is hidden from it. If you say no, we only count visits, anonymously, with no cookies.
  • Your experiment code: only if you say yes. See below.

Your phone also remembers your name, home and choices, so you don't have to type them again. That stays on your phone.

One small cookie, explained

Nobody likes cookies. Well, nobody likes the kind that follow you round the internet. Ours is more of a plain digestive: one crumb, and it stays on this site.

Va Va Voom is an experiment, and you can help us learn. If you say "Yes, count me in", your phone keeps a random code, something like k3f9a01c2b7d4. It lets us see that the same person tapped Growing, then the sauna, then came back on Tuesday. That helps us work out what people round here want, so we can make it better for you.

  • It isn't your name. It's made up at random on your phone. We never put it next to your name, number or email in our records.
  • Only your taps. The code goes with your taps and nothing else.
  • No IP addresses. We don't keep them, here or in PostHog.
  • Never sold, never shared for adverts. Not now, not later.
  • Say no, or nothing, and nothing is stored. Each page you open gets a throwaway code that disappears when you close it.

Changed your mind?

Who sees it

Kev, and the host of anything you've said "I'm in" to, for that event only. An AI helper (Claude, made by Anthropic) also reads what comes in each morning to sort it and spot anything that shouldn't be there. It never contacts you and never shares your details. Other people only see your first name if you tick the box to show it. We never sell your details or share them for adverts.

Where it's kept

In a private Google Sheet and our PostHog account (EU), both protected with two-step login.

How long

While you're involved. If we haven't heard from you in 12 months, we delete your details.

Your rights

You can ask to see, change or delete anything we hold about you, any time, and we'll do it within a month. Tell Kev at any event, or reply to any message we've sent you.

This is an experiment

Things will change as we learn. If how we use your data changes, we'll update this page and tell you.

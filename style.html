House habits

  • Hard ink borders (2 to 3px), square corners, a solid offset shadow on things you press.
  • A slight tilt (about 1 degree) on stamps and cards. Never on text you have to read.
  • Sparks (the four-point stars) after big headings, one per heading.
  • Tap targets at least 44px. Pink dashed outline when you tab to something.
  • UK English, no long dashes, warm and plain.

How they compare

DirectionFeels likeGood forWatch out for
A · Rose riotA gig poster on a café wallStanding out, the playful edge, "not another worthy group"Can read young (Gill). Lots competing for attention.
B · Zine on oatA hand-folded pamphlet passed between friendsCalm, grown-up, lets the words lead. Easiest to keep consistent.Loses some of the voom. Needs strong photos or drawings later.
C · Solarpunk daylightA sunny allotment with good wifiHopeful, welcoming, easy reading for everyone. Fits the solarpunk story.Closer to every other green group. Rounded corners soften the edge.
D · After darkTrinkfluss at 10pmEvent pages, the hidden portals, invitations, social posts.Too much for the main page in daylight. Best as an accent world.

Grain's suggestion

Keep A's attitude, borrow B's calm. That means one spark colour per screen, ink and paper for most of the page, and rose kept for the logo and the one button we want pressed. Use D for the secret portals and event invitations, so going "down the rabbit hole" changes the lights. Try C's reading face on the main page to see if it helps on phones.

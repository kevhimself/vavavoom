Treat yourself this winter

Meet Elenid, and the Waggon Sauna

Elenid Coleman, a textile artist, hosts a wood-fired sauna in a waggon on a woodland walk at the Hampton Estate, Seale. It has a huge window onto the trees, and bucket and woodland showers to cool off. The waggon was built from Hampton's own timber.

  • Go with friends. The Trailblazer pass works out at about £16 a session instead of £20, and you can book friends in on it.
  • Make it a gathering. A private session fits about ten. That's our sauna sing-along idea: sweat, sing badly, feel brilliant.
  • Like it hot? Take the corner seat. Then the bucket. Then do it again.
  • Times change with the seasons. Check the booking page before you go.

Tip from Kev and Jules, who've been and loved it.

Ilona, the VA putting an engine under local business

After ten years as a PA, Ilona went her own way. She walked into pretty much every shop in Godalming, said what she was doing, and was fully booked within a month. Now she's the flexible right hand for small businesses round here, and she joins the dots between them. She's just been on the Trinkfluss Wines podcast with Bastian and Matteo from Take One Studio.

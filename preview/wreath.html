What it isForage, make, gather

A day of making together. We head into the woods to forage natural treasures, then come back to the farm to make a wreath for your door, with tea, coffee, cake and good company.

The question to hold: how do we want to winter, together?

How it goesGather, forage, make

  1. GatherWe open together at the farm, so you've met people before we set off.
  2. ForageInto the woods with a big bag, to collect greenery, leaves, cones and other natural treasures.
  3. MakeBack at the farm (indoors if it's wet) we make our wreaths, with tea, coffee and cake.
  4. CloseWe finish together by 3pm, each with a wreath to take home.

What to bringBags, snips and bits to share

  • A wreath ring, if you have one (a small one is perfect). No ring? We'll have some for £3 if you can.
  • A big bag for collecting what you forage
  • Secateurs, if you have some
  • Things to decorate with: ribbons, feathers, dried fruit, acorns, cones, anything natural from your garden. Bring a few extra to share.
  • Cake, if you're feeling bakey. We'll provide tea and coffee.
  • Warm clothes and boots for the woods

We'll have simple wire to bind it all together.

QuestionsGood to know

I've never made a wreath. Is that OK?

Oh yes. Nobody's an expert here. We'll show you how, and there's no wrong way to do it.

I'm coming on my own. Is that OK?

Very much so. You won't be alone for long.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes, kids are welcome.

What if it rains?

Wrap up for the foraging. The making happens indoors, so it's on whatever the weather.

Does it cost anything?

It's free. If you need a wreath ring, we'll have some for £3 if you can.

Why do I need to book?

It's a small group, up to 12, so it helps us know who to expect, and we can send you directions and a reminder.

Booked, but can't make it after all?

No problem. Reply to your confirmation and let us know, so someone else can have your place.

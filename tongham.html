Good news from round here

Things worth knowing about, right on the doorstep.

Hogs Back Brewery

Surrey's biggest independent brewer is right here, brewing with hops grown at Hampton in Seale. Tours run Fridays to Sundays, £23 with tastings.

Tice's Meadow

An old quarry between Tongham and Badshot Lea turned into a nature reserve. Bring binoculars.

The Rec

A free tennis court (no booking), a basketball court and an outdoor gym, behind the Community Centre.

Treat yourself this winter: the Waggon Sauna

Hosted by Elenid Coleman at the Hampton Estate, Seale. A wood-fired sauna in a waggon, on a woodland walk, with bucket and woodland showers to cool off.

  • Open: Thursday 4 to 8pm, Friday 7 to 11am (the early commuter dip), Saturday 4 to 8pm, Sunday 8am to noon. Check the booking page, as times change with the seasons.
  • Top tip: get the Trailblazer pass. It works out at about £16 a session instead of £20, and you can book friends in on it.
  • Go with: a friend or three. Take the corner seat if you like it hot.

The Human Bit

A circle on being analogue humans in a digital world

Thursday 12 November 2026 · 7pm to 9pm · The studio on the hill, Catteshall Lane · Free

AI has crept into our work, our homes and our heads faster than any of us expected. Some of it's brilliant. Some of it's unsettling. Most of us haven't had time to stop and ask what we actually want from it.

So let's. One evening, in a circle, sharing real stories: where it's helped, where it's taken over, and where we want to stay properly human. We'll leave with one intentional change each, and maybe a project or two to do together.

Hosted by Kev Self. Kev brings a few weeks of living with an AI team that runs his home, his diary and his business: the good, the weird, and what he's learning about staying embodied.

How it'll go: a grounded check-in (one breath, one word) · a story round · open conversation · one change each to close.

Bring: a story, an open mind, and a bottle if you fancy one.

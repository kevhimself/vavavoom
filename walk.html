What it isA walk with a bit of intention

It's as much about the walk and being out in nature as it is about the conversation. We walk, we notice, and we talk about something that matters round here.

We walk with two questions: what do you need, for yourself? And what do we need, as our local community? Three prompts along the way help us open them up, explore them, and think about what we could do together. At the end we pick what we want to carry forward.

How it goesCheck in, walk, take something forward

  1. A check-inWe start at the farm with a quick check-in, so you've met people before we set off.
  2. The walkOut into the countryside at a gentle pace. Along the way there are invitations to talk, and to swap walking partners. Quiet is fine too.
  3. ClosingBack at the farm (indoors if it's wet) we close our time together and share what we want to take forward.

What to bringDress for a muddy field

Our one golden rule: bring a bottle of water.

  • Shoes or boots you don't mind getting muddy
  • A waterproof and warm layers
  • Your curiosity

QuestionsGood to know

I'm coming on my own. Is that OK?

Oh yes, very much so. You won't be alone for long: the check-in at the start means you've met someone before we've even left the farm.

Can I bring my dog?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome. This is time for you to connect with yourself and others, so ask yourself honestly: will your dog help you, and everyone else, get what you want and need from it? Or will they want to chase every pheasant in Surrey?

Can I bring my kids?

Kids and teens are super welcome. Just make sure they know what they're signing up to: an intentional walk, where we walk, notice and talk. Less theme park, more good conversation in a field.

What if it rains?

You'll get wet! So come dressed for it and enjoy the puddles. If the weather is really bad, we'll call it off, and everyone who's booked will hear from us that morning.

How far and how hard is it?

A gentle pace over 2 to 2½ hours, with stops. Expect mud, it's November. If you have any access needs, tell us when you book and we'll talk it through.

Does it cost anything?

Not a penny. Leave your wallet at home.

Why do I need to book?

It helps us get a sense of who to expect, so we're not counting heads at the gate. It's a small group, up to 15, and booking means we can send you directions and a reminder.

Booked, but can't make it after all?

No problem, life happens. Reply to your reminder and let us know, so someone else can have your place.

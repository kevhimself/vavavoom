From the People's Emergency Briefing

Write to your MP in three steps

If the film got you thinking, one simple thing you can do is let your MP know. It takes about five minutes.

Not sure who your MP is? You're in good company. Plenty of us couldn't name ours until we looked. If you live in Godalming, Shackleford, Norney, Puttenham or Tongham, your MP is Sir Jeremy Hunt (Godalming and Ash). Live somewhere else? Step 2 finds yours from your postcode.
  1. See if your MP has already signed. Put your postcode in at nebriefing.org/parliamentary-call. If they have, a thank-you note still helps.
  2. Send a short email. Go to WriteToThem.com, type your postcode, and paste the letter below. Add one line in your own words: that's what makes it count.
  3. Ask to meet, if you can. MPs hold local surgeries. Ask what they'll do next.

A letter you can copy

Dear [your MP's name], I live in [your village or town]. I recently watched the People's Emergency Briefing on climate and nature with my neighbours in Shackleford. Please back the Parliamentary Call for a live, televised Government briefing on climate and nature, and sign Early Day Motion 93 if you haven't already. [One line about why this matters to you, or to where we live.] I'd welcome your reply on what you'll do next. Yours sincerely, [Your name, address and postcode]

Your MP can only reply to people in their area, so include your address and postcode.

What the words mean

Your MP
The person elected to speak for your area in Parliament. They work for you, and their office reads what local people send.
Constituent
Someone who lives in an MP's area. That's you.
The Parliamentary Call
A public list of MPs who've said the Government should hold a live, televised briefing on climate and nature, a bit like the Covid briefings.
Early Day Motion 93
A short statement MPs can add their name to, to show they agree. This one backs the same televised briefing. Signing it is a public "yes, I support this".
MP's surgery
A local slot where you can meet your MP, or someone from their team, face to face. You usually book ahead.
WriteToThem.com
A free, independent website. Type your postcode and it finds your MP and sends your message for you.
